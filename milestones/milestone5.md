## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

Deploying Tandem to Render with an attached PostgreSQL database went smoothly after configuring environment variables and build scripts. The API endpoints and React frontend integrated as expected across both development and production environments.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

Managing database connection pooling and SSL options across production PostgreSQL on Render required fine-tuning in server environment setup. Additionally, ensuring smooth OAuth redirect handling for protected routes presented edge case considerations.

### 3. What were some of the highlights or achievements that you are most proud of in this project?

We successfully implemented all 10 baseline features, 8 out of 8 custom features, and 5 stretch features. Delivering the custom False Friend similarity calculation and split-direction leaderboards with custom RESTful routes were standout technical achievements.

### 4. Reflecting on your web development journey so far, how have you grown since the beginning of the course?

Our understanding of fullstack architecture has deepened significantly, especially regarding relational database schema design, RESTful controller patterns, state management in React, and automated testing with Playwright.

### 5. Looking ahead, what are your goals related to web development, and what steps do you plan to take to achieve them?

We plan to continue expanding fullstack applications using modern frontend frameworks and distributed backend architecture, exploring advanced caching strategies, WebSocket real-time updates, and containerized deployment workflows.
